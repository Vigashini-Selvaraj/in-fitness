Stay Consistent
Before You Begin

Consistency makes your measurements useful.

Use a flexible measuring tape and record your measurements under similar conditions each time. Focus on long-term changes rather than a single measurement.

What you need

A flexible measuring tape, a notebook or digital tracker, and a few minutes in a comfortable space. Keep the tape flat against the body without pulling it tightly.

Measurement Guide

Know where to measure.

Follow these simple steps to keep your measurements consistent from one check-in to the next.

01

Body Weight

Use a reliable weighing scale and stand still while taking your reading. Record the number in the same unit each time.

USE THE SAME SCALE WHEN POSSIBLE
02

Chest

Place the tape around the fullest part of your chest. Keep the tape level and relaxed without compressing the body.

KEEP THE TAPE LEVEL AROUND THE BODY
03

Waist

Measure around your natural waist area. Keep your posture relaxed and avoid pulling the tape tightly.

MEASURE AT THE NATURAL WAISTLINE
04

Hips

Place the measuring tape around the widest part of your hips and seat. Make sure the tape remains level.

MEASURE AROUND THE WIDEST POINT
05

Upper Arm

Measure around the fullest part of the upper arm. Keep your arm relaxed and use the same arm for future measurements.

KEEP THE ARM RELAXED
06

Thigh

Measure around the fullest part of your upper thigh. Keep the tape horizontal and comfortably fitted.

USE THE SAME MEASUREMENT POINT EACH TIME
07

Calf

Measure around the widest part of your calf while standing naturally and keeping the tape level.

MEASURE AT THE WIDEST POINT
08

Record Everything

Write down each measurement immediately. Recording the date along with your numbers makes future comparisons easier.

DATE + MEASUREMENT = BETTER TRACKING
Measurement Checklist

Before you measure.

A consistent routine helps make your measurements easier to compare over time.

Use a flexible tape

Choose a tape that can comfortably follow body contours.

Keep it level

Make sure the tape does not angle upward or downward.

Do not pull tightly

The tape should sit against the body without squeezing.

Stay relaxed

Keep your normal posture and avoid holding your breath.

Record the date

Always save the date alongside your measurements.

Use the same method

Follow the same measurement points every time.

Tracking Progress

Measure less. Understand more.

Measurements are most useful when viewed as part of your overall fitness journey.

01

Keep it consistent

Try to measure under similar conditions and at similar intervals so your records are easier to compare.

02

Look at trends

A single number does not tell the whole story. Look at changes across several check-ins.

03

Track your journey

Combine measurements with attendance, training, strength, and how you feel during your workouts.
