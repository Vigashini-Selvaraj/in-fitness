This Privacy Policy explains how IN+ FITNESS may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or use our membership and fitness services.

01

Overview

IN+ FITNESS respects the privacy of its members, visitors, and users. We aim to be transparent about the information that may be collected when you interact with our services.

This policy applies to information provided through our website, membership services, booking systems, contact forms, and member dashboard.

Important

This page provides general privacy information for the IN+ FITNESS website and services.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with IN+ FITNESS, information may include:

  • Name and contact details.
  • Account and login information.
  • Membership information.
  • Class and session booking information.
  • Attendance and training information.
  • Information submitted through contact forms.
  • Basic technical and website usage information.
03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide, maintain, and improve the services requested by members and visitors.

  • Manage member accounts.
  • Process membership requests.
  • Manage class and session bookings.
  • Maintain attendance records.
  • Provide relevant training information.
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Improve website functionality.
  • Send important service-related communications.
04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences, support functionality, and understand how visitors interact with the website.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

05

Payments

Where payment functionality is available, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

06

Member Accounts

Members may create accounts to access features such as bookings, attendance information, membership details, and assigned training plans.

Members should keep their account credentials secure and notify the appropriate team if they suspect unauthorized access.

07

Data Sharing

Limited information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate our website and services.

This may include hosting providers, technology services, payment processors, communication services, or other providers required to deliver requested features.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect our users, services, or legal rights.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements, or fulfil legitimate operational purposes.

10

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning information associated with your account.

  • Request access to certain information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Ask questions about how information is used.
  • Withdraw certain communications where applicable.
11

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe information relating to a child has been submitted without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, our team is available to help.
